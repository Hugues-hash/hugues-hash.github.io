About

My route into data science is a little different. I trained as an engineer, worked in flight operations and electrical engineering, and kept gravitating toward the data side of every problem. That pull led me to an MSc in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science at the University of Hull, funded by a Commonwealth Scholarship, a competitive UK Government scholarship awarded on academic merit and leadership potential.

What I care about is not just building a model, but proving it is sound: benchmarking it against a known answer, testing where it holds and where it breaks, and reporting the result honestly. I am now looking for 2027 UK graduate roles in data science, quantitative analysis, risk and machine learning. Open to remote, hybrid or on-site across the UK, with no visa sponsorship required.

Selected projects

Python

Volatility Forecasting & Strategy Backtesting

Six volatility models compared out of sample on 21 years of S&P 500 data, ranked by QLIKE and Diebold-Mariano, turned into a volatility-targeting strategy, and validated as one-day Value-at-Risk models with the Kupiec and Christoffersen tests.

Python

Regime & Drift Detection in Financial Time Series

A two-state Hidden Markov Model labels calm and stressed regimes on the S&P 500, and three drift metrics (PSI, KS, KL) are shown to track a live model's rising error, turning distribution drift into an early warning for model decay.

View on GitHub
C++

Monte Carlo Option Pricer

A C++ pricing engine benchmarked against the closed-form Black-Scholes price, checked for convergence at the theoretical rate, sharpened with antithetic and control-variate variance reduction, with finite-difference Greeks and an optional pybind11 Python binding.
